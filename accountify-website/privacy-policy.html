Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Accountify LLC (“Accountify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit accountifyllc.com, contact us, submit a form, request a consultation, or interact with our advertising.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Name

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business information
  • Services you are interested in
  • Information included in your messages or inquiries

We may also automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser/device information, pages visited, referring website, and other website usage information through cookies and similar technologies.

If you become a client, we may collect additional accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, financial, and business information necessary to provide the requested services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Contact you about our services
  • Schedule consultations
  • Provide and manage our services
  • Communicate with clients and prospective clients
  • Improve our website and services
  • Measure and improve marketing and advertising
  • Maintain website security
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Advertising and Lead Generation

Accountify may advertise through platforms such as Google, Facebook, Instagram, and other advertising platforms.

If you submit your information through an Accountify lead form or advertisement, we may use that information to respond to your inquiry and contact you about the services you requested.

We may also use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies to measure advertising performance and understand website activity.

Third-party advertising platforms may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, understand website traffic, improve user experience, and measure advertising performance.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our website, manage leads, communicate with customers, provide services, analyze website activity, or conduct our business.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, security, or property.

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration.

Because Accountify provides accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and financial services, clients may provide financial or business information when necessary to receive our services.

Please do not submit sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank account details, tax identification numbers, or financial records through general website or advertising lead forms. When such information is required, Accountify may provide an appropriate secure method for submitting it.
