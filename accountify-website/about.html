Our story

Experience in finance. Focused on your business.

Accountify was founded in 2020 by Jyoti Bharadwaj, a finance professional with 20+ years of experience in accounting, finance and business operations.

Before starting Accountify, Jyoti held leadership roles at major financial institutions in India, where she managed SAP-based accounting, financial reconciliations and process improvement initiatives. Her experience across diverse industries gives Accountify a strong foundation in accuracy, structuring and scalable financial processes.
